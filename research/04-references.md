## A. "One big idea" personal sites (concept references)

## E. Building blocks that prototype each direction

**Direction A, "Director's Cut" (video-editor metaphor)**
- Framer templates: [CUTT](https://www.framer.com/community/marketplace/templates/cutt/) (horizontal timeline and playhead nav) · [Timecode](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/timecode/) (reads like an edit)
- [21st.dev Filmstrip Scrub](https://21st.dev/@rmahammad/components/filmstrip-scrub) (spring playhead, frame ticks, timecode, MIT) · [Editframe scrubber docs](https://editframe.com/docs/controls/scrubber) (click-to-seek, drag, zoom, frame snapping)
- [ReelIndex](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/components/reelindex/) / [Montage](https://www.framer.com/community/marketplace/templates/montage/) (hover-to-play project index, keyboard and reduced-motion support)

**Direction B, "Checkout" (commerce metaphor)**
- Tone: Teenage Engineering, plus Dubost's CV as precedent
- Your own BOPIS patterns: compact delivery-or-pickup widget, store card with distance, staged order-tracking cards

**Direction C, "Studio Desk" (objects/OS metaphor)**
- Framer: [Desk template](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/desk/) · [Deskfolio](https://www.framer.com/community/posts/HJ592iA6Bw3d2bhXohm7WQ/) · [Infinite Canvas](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/infinite-canvas/) · [Physics Sticker Wall](https://www.framer.com/community/marketplace/components/physics-sticker-wall/) · [Draggable 3D peel sticker](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/components/draggable-sticker/)
- [daedalOS](https://github.com/DustinBrett/daedalOS) (a full desktop OS in the browser) · [Mariano Pascual's customizable desktop](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/interactive-customizable-desktop)

**Useful in any direction**

## G. Identity material (Indian, personal, specific)

- **Indian Type Foundry's Fontshare** (free): Satoshi, General Sans, Cabinet Grotesk, Clash Display. Several of 2026's trending "soft grotesks" are Indian-made. [ITF announcement](https://www.indiantypefoundry.com/news/introducing-fontshare) · [Abduzeedo overview](https://www.abduzeedo.com/fontshare-free-fonts-100-quality-typefaces-designers)
- **Ek Type** (Devanagari and Latin, open source): [Mukta](https://github.com/girish-dalvi/Ek-Mukta), [Gotu](https://github.com/EkType/Gotu). Use these for a small Marathi/Devanagari signature (सुहास). [Devanagari + Latin typesetting guide](https://www.alphabettes.org/devanagari-typography-101-a-guide-for-typesetting-with-latin/)
- **Indian street lettering**: [HandpaintedType / Hanif Kureshi](https://www.creativeboom.com/resources/why-indias-disappearing-street-lettering-is-unmissable-inspiration-for-designers/), [Sadak typeface](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/sadak). Use for one accent, not the whole site.
- **Saoji**: Nagpur's famously fiery cuisine, 30+ spices ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savji)). This is the source of the "Spice level" idea in `05-interaction-ideas.md`.
