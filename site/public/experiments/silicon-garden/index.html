A garden at the
edge of possibility.

Silicon gives machines a place to think. Here, it gives a tree a place to grow.

The silver canopy carries tiny points of light on tiers of fine branches. Change the tree variation beside the rotation controls to explore Blue filament or the multicolored Prism canopy.

Explore
Drag in any direction to orbit. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Arrow keys rotate the focused garden.
Cultivate
Select “Plant again” to start with a bare wafer. Water it four times to grow the selected tree.
Begin again
The garden opens in bloom. “See it in bloom” lets you explore the mature tree immediately.

A procedural 3D study in particles, iridescence, and a little patience.
